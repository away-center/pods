07 / A few good questions

Before
we begin.

Can we start with one person?+

Yes. A Managed Role starts with one dedicated person and the operating layer around them. Add roles or workflows as the work develops.

What kinds of roles can away Pods support?+

Work across revenue, marketing, creative, customer experience, operations, engineering, AI and automation, founder’s office and e-commerce. These are starting points. Tell us the work and we’ll assess the fit.

Who manages the person or team?+

You keep strategy, priorities and final business outcomes. Day-to-day management can sit with you or include away coordination and QA, depending on the agreed scope.

Where does the team work?+

away Pods currently operates from our Bangalore centre, with workspace, meeting infrastructure and other people on the floor.

What happens if someone leaves?+

away provides replacement support, with documentation and handover built around the work. Bench arrangements and replacement terms are defined in your agreement.
