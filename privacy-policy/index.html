Policy for review

Privacy policy.

A dedicated privacy policy for away Pods is required before we publish the new website. This draft identifies the processing already visible in the website code and the decisions still to be verified.

Draft, not approved for publication. Please provide the responsible legal entity, registered address, privacy contact, service providers, analytics inventory and retention schedule so this can be finalised and legally reviewed.

Information sent through the enquiry form

The form asks for your name, business email, optional company name and a description of your work requirement. The website also collects the landing page and campaign parameters with the submission. These details are used to respond to the enquiry and understand the source of interest.

Optional details and session attribution

If supplied, the enquiry also includes optional country, phone, website, team size, timing, operating model, additional information, sample requests and configurator selections. Campaign parameters and the originating page and CTA are kept in browser session storage to preserve context as you navigate. Names, email addresses and enquiry text are not stored in that session record.

The proposed delivery integration sends enquiry details through the website server to Google Sheets. A submission identifier is used to prevent repeated delivery of the same enquiry. The final notice must confirm the deployed setup, retention and access arrangements.

Storage and processors

The identity of the data controller, hosting and email/CRM processors, retention period, cross-border transfers and contact for privacy requests must be confirmed before this document can serve as a published privacy notice.

Analytics and cookies

Any analytics or advertising tags should be inventoried, disclosed and configured with whatever notices and consent choices are legally required for the visitors we serve.

Contact

Until the legal operator and dedicated privacy contact are approved, visitors can use the Pods contact form. A final policy will display direct legal contact details.
