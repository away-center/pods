08 / Good questions

Before we
begin.

When you build a team abroad, the practical questions matter more than the brochure.

Can we start with one person?

Yes. You can begin by defining one role. It does not have to start as an entire offshore department.

Who chooses the person we hire?

You choose the final hire. away supports the search and assessment process, while we agree how interviews and approvals will work.

Where will the team work?

The initial delivery concept is based at an away centre in Bangalore, India. We will confirm the specific setup for each engagement.

Who handles payroll and legal employment?

The employment structure may be through a client entity or an agreed provider such as an employer-of-record service. The final arrangement and responsibilities will be confirmed in the proposal and contracts.

How do we know what is happening?

We intend to define roles or workflows, responsibilities, and a regular reporting cadence with you. Exact tools, metrics and service commitments are agreed before any engagement begins.

What happens if someone leaves?

Continuity planning, documented context, and replacement arrangements are part of the proposed model. The specific terms will be set out in your agreement.
