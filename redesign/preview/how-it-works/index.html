A simple operating model

Your work, done in India.
Without the extra machinery.

We are designing away Pods around three things: thoughtful hiring, a real workspace, and day-to-day operating visibility.

01

Define

We understand the work, your tools, the outcomes and the desired overlap in working hours.

02

Assess

We design the search and evaluation around critical reasoning, job-specific capability and fit.

03

Set up

Once you choose the person, we set up the agreed workspace, operations and reporting approach.

04

We coordinate the local layer included in your agreement and review what needs improving.
