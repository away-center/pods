People behind the idea

Built by people
who build places to work.

away Pods is an emerging service from away.center, a design-led workspace brand with physical centres in India.

The thinking

A better workday
is infrastructure.

Our founder, Udbhav Jalan, has been working in the shared-workspace industry since 2017. The idea for Pods comes from a simple observation: if a company wants to build a small team in India, the person and the desk are only part of the equation.

We want to bring together hiring, space, everyday working habits, local coordination and a clear division of responsibility under one practical service.

away Pods is still being prepared for launch. Existing away workspace clients are not represented here as Pods customers.
