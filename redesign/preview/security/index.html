A transparent operating model

Trust begins with
clear responsibilities.

The exact legal, security and operating controls will depend on the role and the final agreement. This page explains the questions we believe must be answered before work begins.

Review note: This is a design-stage explainer, not a claim of completed security certification or a contractual assurance. The controls below must be verified against the operating service before publication.

Access to your tools and information

We intend to agree who creates user accounts, what applications the team can use, how access is granted, and what happens when a role changes or ends. The client retains authority over its own systems.

Devices and the workplace

The proposal should define whether equipment is supplied by away or the client, the workspace arrangement, security configuration, and procedures for device return or replacement.

Confidentiality and intellectual property

These responsibilities need to be addressed in the actual contracts, including any employment or EOR arrangements. We will not imply that one general website statement settles IP ownership.

Continuity and reporting

Role documentation, reporting cadence, backup context and replacement provisions will be scoped with each customer. Any specific timelines or guarantees belong in a signed service agreement.
